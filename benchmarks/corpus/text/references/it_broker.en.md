The broker uses a declared catalog and measured cards. The catalog specifies provider, model, context, cost and locality. The cards contain latency, reliability and quality per task. A profile describes what the task needs: capabilities, minimum context, privacy, maximum latency and preference among quality, cost and speed. The broker filters unsuitable models, assigns a score and records the choice in the audit log. If no local model is available, the subagent uses the configured model as a fallback.
